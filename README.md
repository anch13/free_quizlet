# Recall

A free flashcard app for creating study sets and reviewing them at your own pace.

Open `index.html` in a modern browser to get started. Create a set with a title
and at least one term and definition, then choose **Study set** to flip through
its cards. Sets can also be edited or deleted from the collection.

Your sets are stored in the browser's local storage on this device. They are
not synced between browsers or devices and may be removed if you clear your
browser data.
